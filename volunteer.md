Join other parents and get involved with your school community. Everything we do is made possible by our dedicated parent volunteers.

## Volunteer opportunities

Here's where we could use a hand right now:

<div class="boxed-list-item" markdown="1">
🍣 Sushi Sensei
{: .boxed-list-item-heading}

After years of running Sushi Lunch, Usha is officially retiring on December 15! We need new dedicated volunteers to help keep the lunch rolling.

- When: Tuesdays, 11:30 am – 1 pm (shared with others).
- What: Oversee setup, lunch distribution, and cleanup.
- Experience: None! Training and support provided.
</div>

<div class="boxed-list-item" markdown="1">
🍕 Slice Specialists
{: .boxed-list-item-heading}

Our Pizza Lunch raised over $20,000 last year! Not bad for cheese and pepperoni. We need committed volunteers to come join in the fun.

- When: Thursdays, 11:30 am – 1 pm, as little as once every two months!
- What: Help oversee pizza distribution alongside a seasoned specialist.
- Experience: We'll train you! Enthusiasm for pizza optional.
</div>

<div class="boxed-list-item" markdown="1">
🚲 School Streets Lead
{: .boxed-list-item-heading}

Help keep L'École Bilingue's School Streets program running smoothly this spring (May 2027) by coordinating parent volunteers and being the go-to link between our school, the VSB, and the City of Vancouver. Good news: you don't need to work the physical shifts yourself! The role mainly involves a bit of organizing and communication, with a few optional Zoom working group meetings in the new year. The time commitment can be very low and flexible.

Co-leads are preferred (two heads are better than one!), but a solo lead is absolutely welcome too.

And you won't be doing it alone! Previous leads Heather and Sarah will be around to share their knowledge and help ensure a smooth transition.
</div>
