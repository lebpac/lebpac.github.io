Mark your calendars—the much-anticipated Welcome Back BBQ is happening on **Thursday, September 24, from 5–8 pm** following _Meet the Teacher_.

This annual event is both an important fundraiser and a wonderful opportunity to come together as a community, and ALL are welcome!
