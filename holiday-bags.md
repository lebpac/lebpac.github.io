For the ninth year, our school community is preparing holiday gift bags for the Downtown Community Health Centre on Powell Street, which supports individuals on the Downtown Eastside. For many clients visiting during the last week of the year, this may be their only gift. Our support truly matters.

## How to help

We’re asking divisions to collect **100 of their assigned items** (see the list below) so we can once again fill 100 gift bags. Students can drop off items in the blue IKEA bags in their pod.

**The deadline for collection is Friday, December 11.**

In addition, the whole school community is invited to donate:
